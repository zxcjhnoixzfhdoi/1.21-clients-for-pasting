# Skeletons

Draws bone lines over player models, through walls.

---

### Color Mode

Sets the colour used for the bones.

- **TEAM**: uses the dyed colour of the target's helmet
- **HEALTH**: shifts from green at full health through yellow to red at low health
- **STATIC**: uses a fixed colour of your choice

Humanoid mobs have no team or health colour worth reading, so they always use **Color** whichever
mode is selected.

### Team Only

When enabled, players without a dyed helmet get no skeleton at all in **TEAM** mode. When
disabled, they fall back to **Color**. Only visible in **TEAM** mode.

### Render Self

Draw your own skeleton. Only ever visible in third person, since your model is not rendered in
first person.

### Players Only

Restrict the skeleton to players. Turn this off to draw one over any humanoid mob as well, such
as zombies, skeletons, and piglins.

### Ignore Target Filter

When enabled, every other player gets a skeleton instead of only those matching the global
**Target Filter** rules. Only visible when **Players Only** is enabled.

### Line Width

Thickness of the bone lines.

### Color

Colour of the bones in **STATIC** mode, and the fallback for helmetless players in **TEAM** mode.
Supports theme, RGB, and chroma like every other colour in the client. Its alpha carries into
**HEALTH** and **TEAM** too, so the transparency slider still applies in every mode.
