# Auto Eat

Eats when you get hungry, and gaps when you get low

Auto Eat holds use for you when your food bar drops, and reaches for a golden apple
instead when your health drops. It only ever picks from your hotbar.

---

### Food filter

Chooses which items count as food, using the same item picker as every other whitelist
in the client. Defaults to the **Food** category.

Switch the picker to blacklist to eat anything edible *except* the entries you list. An
empty whitelist matches nothing; an empty blacklist matches everything.

### Hunger

Food level to start eating at. Eating begins once your food bar is at or below this.

### Restore slot

Switch back to the slot you were holding once the item is finished.

## Auto Gapple

### Auto gapple

Reach for a golden apple when your health drops, independently of hunger. Gapples take
priority over the food filter.

### Gapple health

Health to start eating a gapple at.

### Enchanted only

Only use enchanted golden apples. With this off it prefers enchanted ones and falls back
to normal golden apples.

### Avoid players

Don't start eating while another player is nearby.

### Player range

How close a player has to be to block eating.

### Damage ticks

Don't start eating if you have lost health within this many ticks.
