# Misplace

Pulls players toward you so you can hit from further away

---

### Mode

Normal misplace will flag the same as a server's reach checks.
Understand [AntiCheat safety](/guides/anticheat-safety#risk-labels)
before configuring this module.

- **NORMAL**: drags nearby players toward you for the moment the crosshair is picked, so a
  target out past your reach is picked as though it were in range
- **LAG**: holds your own outgoing packets for a moment after you land a hit, so the server sees
  the hit before it sees you moving away from it

### Only players

Ignore mobs and other entities.

---

## Normal

### Amount

How much closer to pull them, in blocks. This is your extra reach, so 0.1 means you hit at 3.1 reach.

### Max range

Players beyond this are left alone.

### Disadvantage

Pushes them away instead of pulling them in, costing you reach rather than giving it. The offset
is wound down as a player closes on you either way, so it can never drag a hitbox through you.

---

## Lag

### Lag window

Low end is the window a single hit opens. Each hit after that adds 50ms until the high end.

### Min distance

Never lag for a target nearer than this. The default of 3.1 keeps it out of normal reach
entirely.

### Drain per tick

How many held packets to release each tick once the window closes. Releasing the whole backlog
at once puts several moves into one server tick. Higher catches back up sooner and flags
more, 1 is the safest and takes the longest to resync.
