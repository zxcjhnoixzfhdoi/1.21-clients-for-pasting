# Powder Chest Solver

Finds powder chests and aims at their lock-picking particle.

---

The solver locks onto one chest at a time, follows its lock-picking particles,
and releases its rotation after the chest is solved. **Nuker** is paused while a
chest is being solved.

### Legit mode

Only selects the powder chest currently under your crosshair. When disabled,
the nearest valid powder chest within range is selected automatically.

### Smooth rotations

Smoothly rotates toward each lock-picking particle instead of snapping.

### Rotation speed

Sets how quickly smooth rotations move toward the particle.

### Chest color

Sets the colour used to highlight the selected powder chest.

### Particle color

Sets the colour used to highlight the detected lock-picking particle.
