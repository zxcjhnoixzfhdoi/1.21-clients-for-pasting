# Clutch

Saves yourself from falling.

---

### Trigger

Chooses when clutching should activate. The drop is measured from where the
simulation says you will land, not from a straight line down.

- **ALWAYS**: always attempt a clutch
- **ON VOID**: only when the simulation runs out of world without landing
- **ON LETHAL FALL**: only when the drop would do more damage than you have
  health, or you are heading into the void
- **FALL DISTANCE**: only when the drop is deeper than **Blocks**, whether or
  not it would kill you

### Blocks

Minimum drop depth, in blocks, for FALL DISTANCE mode.

### Only on combat damage

Only arm after you have been hit recently, so it stays out of the way when you jump off
something deliberately. Ignored: fall damage, fire and lava, drowning, freezing, starving,
the void, and standing in cactus, sweet berries, magma or powder snow.

### Damage window (ticks)

How long Clutch stays armed after being hit.

## Behaviour

### Auto ladder

Fall back to a ladder rescue when a block path cannot reach in time. Needs
ladders in your hotbar, which are then excluded from normal clutch blocks.

### Allow staircase up

Repeated jumps in quick succession queue a staircase, letting Clutch catch you
one level higher than the plain fall would.

### Search depth

How many blocks Clutch may chain together to reach the catch spot.

### Search radius

How wide a ring around your predicted position Clutch sweeps for blocks to
build off. The sweep is biased along the way you are moving.

### Fail delay (ms)

How long to wait before trying to find a route again after a failure.

### Limit blocks

Refuse a clutch that would need more than **Max blocks**.

### Clutch move delay

Ticks of movement freeze after a clutch completes, so you do not immediately
walk back off the edge.

## Rotations

### Speed

Maximum flick speed, in degrees per tick, when placing blocks.

### Reset angle

Look back to your original angle once the clutch is done.

### Reset angle delay

Ticks to wait before resetting the angle.

## Items

### Return to last slot

Select the slot you were holding before the clutch once it completes.

### Return delay

Ticks to wait before restoring the slot.

### Blacklist

Do not clutch with these blocks.

### Held whitelist

Only clutch while holding a whitelisted block, and only use the held block.

### Show block count

Show the number of blocks left in the modules list, or the number of placements
remaining while a clutch is running.
